# Anonimus visual motifs (locked)

The homepage hero is the Signet Token only.
Do not put a sphere, torus, crescent canvas, gold donut, node-head, or face on the landing hero.

## 1. Signet Token (hero, shipped)

A blank credential token, turning in the void.
CylinderGeometry. Diameter : thickness = 10 : 1.
Face A (front): #14161A, empty, inner hairline #3A3D44.
Face B (back): #14161A + recessed interrupted circle in #C6A35A.
Rim: #C6A35A.
Slow yaw, 16s per turn. Cursor tilt ≤ 0.15 rad.
Typography leads. Token is the mark beside the type.

## 2. Blank Signet (locked for success / receipt states)

Not for the homepage hero.
A thin dark token, charcoal face #14161A, electrum rim #C6A35A.
The face is blank except one pressed mark: the interrupted circle (same as the wordmark).
No portrait, no initials, no biometric.
Use on verification success, campaign registration success, and receipt surfaces.
Motion: one slow settle as if a stamp just lifted. Then rest.

## 5. Visor fragment (locked for concealment moments)

Not for the homepage hero. Not a mask.
A curved dark shell, convex, no eyeholes, no nose, no mouth.
Gold exists only as an edge #C6A35A.
From the front it is a shield. From the side it is a fragment.
Use sparingly on disclosure, "what this room receives," or privacy empty-states.
If eyeholes appear, the motif is wrong. Delete them.

## Forbidden hero objects

Glass sphere, disco sphere, gold torus, flat letter-C ring, crescent canvas,
wireframe head, node bust, Venetian mask, Guy Fawkes, fingerprint, DNA, eye scan, passport.
